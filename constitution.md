# AgTech constitution

Starter draft.

- **AG-1** Offline-first: core flows work without connectivity, and sync resolves conflicts safely. (Test)
- **AG-2** Built for low-end Android devices and poor networks; app-size and payload budgets are enforced in CI. (Policy)
- **AG-3** Local languages and low-literacy UX: icons and voice prompts, no text-only critical flows. (Rubric)
- **AG-4** Farmers own their farm and land data; it is shared only with explicit, revocable consent. (Test, Rubric)
- **AG-5** Crop recommendations show their confidence and the reasons behind them. (Rubric)
- **AG-6** All payments and payouts go through APX. (Policy)
